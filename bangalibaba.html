Bangali Baba🍋🌶️

He does not sleep. He only waits behind the smoke.

Who Is Bangali Baba

Deep in the crumbling lanes behind the old jute mill, past the banyan tree whose roots are said to have swallowed three separate roads over the decades, there is a door that is never fully closed. Behind it lives a man the neighborhood no longer names aloud — they simply call him Bangali Baba.

No one remembers when he arrived. The oldest resident on the street, a woman well into her nineties, insists he looked exactly the same when she was a child — same clouded eyes, same grey-streaked beard, same low laugh that seems to come from somewhere behind you rather than in front. He keeps no calendar. He keeps no clock. Time, in his room, is said to move differently — visitors who stay for what feels like minutes often return home to find hours, sometimes an entire evening, missing from their lives.

☠ ✦ ☠

The Room Behind The Curtain

His room smells permanently of scorched hair and clove smoke. A single oil lamp burns at all hours, though no one has ever seen it refilled. The walls are lined with photographs of people no one recognizes — some of them, visitors swear, blink when you're not looking directly at them. In the corner sits a brass bowl that is never empty, no matter how much ash is scraped from it.

He speaks rarely, and when he does, his voice arrives half a second before his lips move — a detail almost everyone notices and almost no one mentions out loud, as if saying it might make it more real than it already is.

☠ ✦ ☠

What He Keeps

The Red Thread

Tied only after midnight. Those who wear it say their dreams stop belonging to them.

The Ash Bowl

Said to hold the remains of every promise ever broken in his presence.

The Second Shadow

Visitors sometimes leave with one more shadow than they arrived with. It fades in a week. Usually.

The Silent Bell

It has no clapper, yet is heard ringing on the nights someone in the lane passes away.

☠ ✦ ☠

Before You Knock

They say he only answers the door for those who were already going to knock, whether they meant to or not. If you find yourself standing before that door with no memory of walking there — do not look behind you. And whatever he offers you, remember: nothing leaves that room for free.
